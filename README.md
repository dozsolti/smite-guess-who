# Smite Guess Who

## Play Now

https://dozsolti.github.io/smite-guess-who/

## How to play
1. **Send the link to a friend**: This game requires two players.

2. **Choose your god**: You and your friend each choose a god.

3. **Ask yes/no questions**: Ask questions like "Is your god a Guardian?" or "Does your god do magical damage?"

4. **Eliminate gods**: Based on the answers, gods that don't match will be grayed out

5. **Repeat**: Once someone guessed press the "New Game" button in the top right corner.
